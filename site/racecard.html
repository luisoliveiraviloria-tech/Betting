How picks are made (mkt-fav-v0). For every UK/IRE race: best bookmaker price per runner (or the published betting forecast before books open) → implied chance, normalised so each race sums to 100%. Pick the favourite if its price is 4.0 or shorter, at most 5 a day, strongest first, £2 flat stakes on a £100 bank (the repo's production staking for that bank size). No proposals if the bank falls below £20. This is the market baseline, not an edge: backing favourites lost 3.9% of stakes over 2017+ in the repo's own backtest. The ledger assumes each pick was placed at its pick price unless you untick it or enter the price you took. Source: sportinglife.com. Chance % is market-implied, not a model forecast.
